{# Coach chat panel (docs/designs/coach-chat.md, DS-1..DS-10; DESIGN.md "Coach chat"). ONE element on every screen size. >= 768px it sits in the Plan page's left column, behind the Generate | Coach toggle. Below that it is a fixed one-row composer above the tab bar that sheet.js turns into a bottom sheet. It is never moved in the DOM, so the textarea keeps its focus and text (iOS drops the keyboard when a focused input moves). Everything dynamic is built by app/static/coach_chat.js with createElement/textContent. #}

Before you chat

Your messages, your coach notes and a summary of your training are sent to Google's Gemini API to write replies.

On Google's free tier, Google may use this text to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Google asks that free-tier users don't send sensitive personal information, so keep health details brief. Deleting a plan or a note removes it from Zenkai, not from Google.
